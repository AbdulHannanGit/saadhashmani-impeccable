Built through execution, not theory.

Karachi to Houston, from zero and back again. Eight moments, 1989 to 2026. Drag the line to move through them.

  1. 1989–2008

    Karachi Roots

    Origin

    Born in Karachi to a middle-class family that taught him early that “without education there is no way out.” A tough school transfer became the fire behind a lifelong work ethic, and in 2008 he enrolled in mechanical engineering at NED University.

  2. 2010–2013

    A New Country

    Foundation

    After 12 US visa rejections and one from the UK, the family finally emigrated to Texas, arriving with no house, no car, and no career. While studying engineering at Texas Tech, he built a first business from scratch: an eBay reselling operation that reached Top Rated Seller status before a brand dispute forced him to pivot to reselling used electronics door to door.

  3. 2014–2015

    The Phone Guys

    Operator

    Left engineering unfinished at the end of 2013 despite a strong GPA, driving home overnight to start over. Opened his first retail store, The Phone Guys, then a second in Austin. Both ran at a loss for years while he lived frugally, saving and reinvesting every dollar.

  4. 2016

    The Breakthrough

    Growth

    A franchise partnership turned the business around, the deal famously closed over a steak dinner in a rented suit and a rented car. The win opened the door to real estate expansion alongside a growing retail footprint across Texas.

  5. 2021

    Rebuilding From Zero

    Resilience

    Everything he had built fell, from 100% to 40%. What followed was roughly two and a half years of genuine, unglamorous rebuilding, grounded this time in a decade of real operating experience rather than appearances.

  6. 2024

    Sharing The Knowledge

    Education

    Launched The H University, a free skills and e-commerce education platform for aspiring entrepreneurs, funded out of pocket with a personal $100,000 investment and no donations taken.

  7. 2025

    Market Education

    Trading

    Launched Trading Papa, a crypto and forex education platform built on a $300,000 investment, extending the mission from business skills to financial markets with free signals and live coaching.

  8. 2026

    Backing The Next Generation

    Investing

    The H University surpassed 400,000 students. Now putting his own capital, banking relationships, and audience behind an SME investment collaboration to help fund and build the next wave of entrepreneurs.
